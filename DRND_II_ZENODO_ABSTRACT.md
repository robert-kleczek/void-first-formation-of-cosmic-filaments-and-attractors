## Title

Void-First Formation of Cosmic Filaments and Attractors: SDSS, 2M++, and Peculiar-Velocity Validation Tests of a DRND Topological-Ejection Scenario

## Abstract / Description

This repository-ready package, version v.1.0.0, presents a reproducible validation package for an experimental Discrete Relational Network Dynamics (DRND) concept in which cosmic filaments and attractor nodes are secondary structures generated by the growth and collision of void compensation shells. In this void-first chronology, early local overdensities and protocluster gas streams are allowed, but the mature global cosmic web emerges only after underconnected void domains form and begin to expel or redistribute matter toward shell ridges.

The package combines four observational tests. First, an SDSS void-lensing amplitude comparison shows that the locked DRND topological gain amplitude, based on the shell gain η = 19/2, is within 0.5 per cent of the same-geometry free amplitude and improves BIC over a zero-signal null by ΔBIC = 74.95. Second, SDSS DR8 filament axes show a statistically robust radial-axis excess relative to the compensated void shell: for high shell-score filament blocks, <|cos θ|> = 0.54546 with a 95 per cent bootstrap interval [0.54013, 0.55075] against the isotropic expectation 0.5. Third, the Carrick/2M++ reconstructed velocity field gives a conditional kinematic consistency signal: voids classified as void-in-void show positive monopole outflow at the DRND shell and a stronger positive outflow at 2.5Rv, while compressed or compensated voids show infall. Fourth, strict line-of-sight peculiar-velocity controls using SDSS PV and Cosmicflows-4 do not recover a positive outflow signal, and are therefore retained as negative controls.

The package explicitly separates prior observational phenomenology from the proposed DRND mechanism. Hierarchical void growth, void-in-void and void-in-cloud evolution, Dipole-Repeller-like kinematic descriptions, and topological skeleton extraction are treated as established background. The novelty claimed here is narrower: DRND proposes a causal bridge from phase-degraded relational domains and MFPT-like transport bottlenecks to macroscopic shell displacement with locked gain η = 19/2, making filaments and attractors secondary compression products of void-shell front collisions.

The package also states a high-redshift falsification boundary. JWST detections of local filamentary protostructures around quasars or protoclusters do not by themselves falsify the void-first model, because these are biased overdense environments rather than mature void-bounded filaments. The decisive test is whether a global, SDSS-like foam of large voids and thin separating filaments exists at z > 6 before void domains could form. If such a mature void-and-filament architecture is observed, the void-first DRND chronology is strongly falsified. If the shell-alignment and void-in-void outflow signatures persist under matched Lambda-CDM mocks and high-redshift Lyman-alpha tomography, the concept becomes a viable competing formation channel for the cosmic web.

This package includes the manuscript PDF and LaTeX source, processed evidence tables, velocity-field summaries, Bayesian/BIC proxy tables, figures, scripts, data-source manifest, and SHA256 file manifest. The results are presented as a falsifiable technical preprint, not as a final proof that Lambda-CDM is falsified.
